An addon that measures how fast you are actually levelling — counting the deaths, the corpse runs and the time stood in a bank, because that is how levelling really goes. It also tracks who keeps killing you in battlegrounds, and which rares are being killed on your server.

Free, open source, and works on any 3.3.5a private server. No account, no sign-up, no email. checking the board…

Levelling

  • XP per hour, time to next level, mobs to go
  • Which quests in your log are worth doing — measured against how long they are actually taking you
  • Rested XP handled properly

Nemesis

  • Live meter of how you rank against your own team
  • Who joined, who left, and when your arch nemesis turns up
  • Flags, kills, deaths, killstreaks, 14 achievements

Rare Finder

  • 420 rares, recognised by creature id — works in any client language
  • Kills by people near you count too
  • Learns your server's custom rares by itself

Getting it

  1. Download 1-ADDON-LevelPace.zip and unzip it into Interface\AddOns. Ignore the "Source code" files — GitHub attaches those to everything.
  2. Start WoW. A black and purple button appears on your minimap. Everything is behind it.
  3. To appear on the board below: unzip 2-UPLOADER-LevelPace-Companion.zip anywhere and run LevelPace Companion.bat, then tick Share to leaderboard on the minimap menu and type /reload.

Nothing leaves your computer until you tick that box. Your stats upload after you log out or /reload — WoW only writes its saved data at those moments, and no addon can make it happen sooner.

This ranks LevelPace users, not the server. Nobody appears here unless they installed the addon, switched sharing on, and ran the companion. A 100 parse means best among the people on this board — it is not a claim about anyone else. There is no alternative: none of this is recorded server-side, so the data exists only because the addon measured it while someone played.

Every number is supplied by the player's own client. The addon writes a file, the companion sends it. Nothing is verified against the game server, because nothing can be. Impossible values are rejected and nobody can submit under someone else's name, but plausible lies are not detectable. Treat this as a scoreboard among friends, not an audited ranking.

Something broken? An idea?

Say so at github.com/monscorps/levelpace/issues — there are two short forms, one for bugs and one for ideas. Or type /lp feedback in game and the link prints in chat.

If it is the uploader misbehaving, right-click its tray icon → Copy log (for Discord) and paste that in. It is already formatted, and it is the difference between a fix and a guessing game. Ideas are as welcome as bugs — say what you want to know or do, rather than how you think it should work.

Things it deliberately cannot do
  • Stats only upload after you log out or /reload. WoW writes its saved data at those moments and no addon can change that.
  • Enemy guild names are partial. The game only reveals the guild of someone you have targeted or moused over, so it shows "guilds known: 6 of 15" rather than pretending the rest have none.
  • Flag announcements are English only. 3.3.5a ships no translatable text for flag events.
  • Battleground win/loss counts from when you installed, not your whole career. Only honorable kills are genuinely lifetime, because only those come from the server.
  • A match you leave early is not recorded — the winner is only known when it ends.
  • 3.3.5a only. Retail and the Classic clients changed both the combat log and the GUID format, so it would not merely misbehave there — it would silently record nothing.
