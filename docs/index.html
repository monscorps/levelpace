Fine print
  • Ranks LevelPace users, not the server. 100 = best among the people here.
  • Every number is client-reported. Impossible values are rejected; plausible lies are not detectable.
  • Stats upload after logout or /reload — WoW writes saved data only then.
  • Battleground W/L counts from install. Honorable kills are true lifetime.
  • Enemy guilds show as "6 of 15": the game only reveals guilds of people you targeted.
  • 3.3.5a only. Elsewhere it would record nothing, not misbehave.
