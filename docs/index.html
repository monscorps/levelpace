Three steps

    Unzip the addon

    1-ADDON-LevelPace.zip into Interface\AddOns.

    Ignore the "Source code" files. GitHub attaches those to everything.

  2. 2

    Look at your minimap

    A purple button appears. Everything is behind it.

    That is the whole interface. Slash commands still work.

  3. 3

    Join the board

    Run the companion, tick Share to leaderboard, type /reload.

    Optional. Nothing leaves your computer until you tick it.

This ranks LevelPace users, not the server. Nobody appears unless they installed the addon and switched sharing on. A 100 parse means best among the people here. None of this is recorded server-side — it exists only because the addon measured it.

Every number comes from the player's own client. Impossible values are rejected and nobody can submit under someone else's name, but plausible lies are not detectable. A scoreboard among friends, not an audited ranking.

Broken? Got an idea?

Open an issue → Two short forms — one for bugs, one for ideas. Or type /lp feedback in game and the link prints in chat.

If it is the uploader misbehaving, right-click its tray icon → Copy log and paste that in. It is the difference between a fix and a guessing game. Ideas are as welcome as bugs.

Things it deliberately cannot do
  • Stats upload only after you log out or /reload. WoW writes its saved data at those moments; no addon can change that.
  • Enemy guild names are partial. The game only reveals the guild of someone you targeted, so it shows "6 of 15" rather than pretending the rest have none.
  • Flag announcements are English only. 3.3.5a ships no translatable text for flag events.
  • Battleground win/loss counts from when you installed, not your whole career. Only honorable kills are genuinely lifetime.
  • 3.3.5a only. Retail and Classic changed both the combat log and the GUID format — it would not misbehave there, it would silently record nothing.
