LevelPace — what to do
======================

Two things. That is all.


1. THE ADDON
------------

Unzip LevelPace.zip into your WoW folder so you end up with:

    ...\Interface\AddOns\LevelPace\LevelPace.toc

If you see LevelPace\LevelPace\LevelPace.toc you went one folder too deep.
Move it up one.

Start WoW. At the character screen click AddOns and make sure LevelPace is
ticked. If it looks greyed out, tick "Load out of date AddOns".

In game, type:  /lp

That is the addon done. It works completely on its own from here -- XP
tracking, quest rankings, kill streaks, achievements, all of it. You never
need anything else.


2. THE COMPANION  (only if you want to be on the leaderboard)
-------------------------------------------------------------

Double-click:

    LevelPace Companion.bat

That is the whole instruction. Nothing installs, nothing asks you anything.

An icon appears in your notification area, bottom-right by the clock. It may
be hidden under the little ^ arrow -- drag it out if you want it visible.

Leave it running. It uploads your stats by itself whenever you log out of
WoW, and brings everyone else's rankings back into your addon.

Right-click the icon for:

    Upload now           do it immediately instead of waiting
    Open the web board   the rankings in a browser
    View log             what it has actually been doing
    Quit LevelPace       close it

Want it to start with Windows?
  Press Win+R, type  shell:startup  and press Enter.
  Right-click "LevelPace Companion.bat" -> Create shortcut, and drag the
  shortcut into the folder that opened.

Before anything uploads you must turn sharing on, once:

    /lp  ->  Leaderboard  ->  tick "Share my levelling stats"

For the twink board and nemesis rankings, also tick "Also share PvP stats".
That one is separate on purpose: it includes the names of players who killed
you, and they never agreed to anything.


WHY IT ONLY UPDATES WHEN YOU LOG OUT
------------------------------------

WoW writes its saved data to disk when you log out or type /reload -- never
while you are playing. No addon can change that; there is no button for it.

So the companion watches that file and uploads within a second or two of the
game writing it. In practice: play, log out, and it is sent before you have
finished looking at the character screen.

The addon itself tracks everything live in game. This is only about getting
it out.

To see new rankings in game, /reload after the companion has run. The board
tells you how old it is at the top right, so you always know.


SEEING THE RANKINGS
-------------------

    /lp board

    Overall   who is levelling fastest, scored 0-100 like a WarcraftLogs parse
    Twinks    kills this week, item level, streaks, k/d, your top nemesis
    My PvP    your own record -- works even if you share nothing


WHAT GETS SENT
--------------

Only if you ticked the box. Per completed level: the level, how long it took,
XP by source, kills, quests, deaths, corpse-run time. Plus a display name and
a random id.

With PvP sharing on: kills, deaths, item level, streaks, achievements, and
your top three nemeses.

Never: where you are, quest names, who you group with, chat, or anything
about your account. Nothing that could log into anything.

Untick the box and the addon deletes its export immediately.


SOME NUMBERS ARE BEST GUESSES, AND IT SAYS SO
---------------------------------------------

Not everything WoW 3.3.5a shows you is available to an addon. Where something
had to be reconstructed it is labelled rather than dressed up:

  Kills and streaks   EXACT. The game tells your client when you kill
                      someone, with their name.

  Nemesis             A GUESS. Nothing tells you who killed YOU -- the game
                      only tells the killer. So it blames whoever last hit
                      you. It gets ganks and falls wrong.

  Weekly kills        RECONSTRUCTED. There is no weekly counter in this
                      version of WoW. It starts from zero when you install,
                      so your first week looks low. Honest, not broken.

  Item level          Heirlooms are left out. The game reports them as item
                      level 1, which would wreck the average.

  Time to level       Counts everything -- deaths, corpse runs, standing
                      about. It is meant to. Go AFK and it TELLS you the
                      estimate includes a gap rather than hiding it.


IF SOMETHING LOOKS WRONG
------------------------

  /lp debug                    last 20 XP events the addon saw
  right-click icon -> View log what the companion has been doing

Send whoever gave you this both of those. Worth saying whether you were in a
group or a raid at the time -- it changes what the game sends the addon.
