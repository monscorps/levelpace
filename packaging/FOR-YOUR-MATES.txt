LevelPace — what to do
======================

Two downloads. One is a WoW addon, one is not. That trips everyone up, so:

  LevelPace.zip              -> goes in your AddOns folder
  LevelPace-Leaderboard.zip  -> goes ANYWHERE ELSE. Not AddOns. Desktop is fine.


IF YOU JUST WANT THE ADDON
--------------------------

You do not need the second download at all. The addon on its own tracks your
XP, tells you which quests are worth doing, and records your PvP kills,
streaks and achievements. All of that works offline, forever, alone.

  1. Unzip LevelPace.zip into:
       C:\<your WoW folder>\Interface\AddOns\

     You should end up with:
       ...\Interface\AddOns\LevelPace\LevelPace.toc

     If you see LevelPace\LevelPace\LevelPace.toc you went one folder too
     deep. Move it up one.

  2. Start WoW. At the character screen click AddOns and make sure LevelPace
     is ticked. If it looks greyed out, tick "Load out of date AddOns".

  3. In game type  /lp

Useful commands:

  /lp          settings (colours, fonts, layout, what to show)
  /lp board    the leaderboard and your PvP record
  /lp quests   which of your quests are worth doing right now
  /lp reset    start this level's tracking over


IF YOU WANT TO BE ON THE LEADERBOARD
------------------------------------

Two extra steps, then it looks after itself.

  4. In game:  /lp  ->  Leaderboard  ->  tick "Share my levelling stats"
     For the twink board and nemesis rankings, also tick "Also share PvP
     stats". That one is separate on purpose: it includes the names of
     players who killed you, and they never agreed to anything.

  5. Log out, or type /reload.

     This matters. WoW only writes its saved data when you log out or
     reload -- never while you are playing. Nothing exists to send until
     you do one of those.

  6. Unzip LevelPace-Leaderboard.zip somewhere (Desktop is fine) and
     double-click:

       run-uploader.bat

     Nothing to install. It uses the PowerShell that already comes with
     Windows.

Want to see exactly what gets sent before sending anything?
  Double-click  run-uploader-dryrun.bat  -- it prints it and sends nothing.

Do not want to remember step 6 every time?
  Double-click  run-uploader-auto.bat  and leave the window open. It uploads
  by itself whenever you finish playing. That file explains how to make it
  start with Windows.


SEEING THE RANKINGS
-------------------

  /lp board

Three tabs:

  Overall   who is levelling fastest, scored 0-100 like a WarcraftLogs parse
  Twinks    kills this week, item level, streaks, k/d, your top nemesis
  My PvP    your own record -- works even if you share nothing

The board updates when the uploader runs AND you /reload. It tells you how
old it is at the top right, so you always know whether you are looking at
something current.


WHAT GETS SENT, EXACTLY
-----------------------

Only if you ticked the box. Per completed level: the level, how long it took,
XP by source, kills, quests, deaths, corpse-run time. Plus a display name and
a random id.

If you also ticked PvP: kills, deaths, item level, streaks, achievements, and
your top three nemeses.

Never sent: where you are, quest names, who you group with, chat, anything
about your account, your email, your password. Nothing that could log into
anything.

Changed your mind? Untick the box -- the addon deletes its export
immediately. To be removed from the board as well, ask whoever runs it.


A FEW NUMBERS ARE BEST GUESSES, AND THE ADDON SAYS SO
-----------------------------------------------------

Not everything WoW 3.3.5a shows you is actually available to an addon. Where
something had to be reconstructed, it is labelled rather than dressed up:

  Kills and streaks     EXACT. The game tells your client when you kill
                        someone, with their name.

  Nemesis               A GUESS. Nothing tells you who killed YOU -- the
                        game only tells the killer. So it blames whoever
                        last hit you. It gets ganks and falls wrong.

  Weekly kills          RECONSTRUCTED. There is no weekly counter in this
                        version of WoW. It starts from zero when you install
                        and counts up from there, so your first week looks
                        low. That is honest, not broken.

  Item level            Heirlooms are left out. The game reports them as
                        item level 1, which would wreck the average.

  Time to level         Counts everything -- deaths, corpse runs, standing
                        about. It is meant to. If you go AFK it will TELL you
                        the estimate includes a gap rather than quietly
                        hiding it.


ACHIEVEMENTS
------------

Fourteen, earned from real events, announced once in chat:

  First Blood, Ten Count, Century            kills
  On A Roll, Untouchable, Unstoppable        streaks of 5, 10, 25
  Bloodbath                                  5 kills in a minute
  Revenge                                    kill whoever killed you last
  Nemesis Down                               kill someone who got you 3+ times
  Even Score                                 draw level with a 5+ nemesis
  Arch-Rival                                 10 kills each way with one player
  Humbled                                    die to the same player 10 times
  Well Rounded                               kill 10 different players
  Kitted Out                                 item level 40 under level 20

See them under  /lp board  ->  My PvP


IF SOMETHING IS WRONG
---------------------

  /lp debug     prints the last 20 XP events it saw

Tell whoever gave you this what you typed and what happened. Worth mentioning
whether you were in a group or a raid at the time -- it changes what the game
sends the addon.
