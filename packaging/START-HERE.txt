LevelPace Companion
===================

*** THIS IS NOT THE ADDON. DO NOT PUT THIS FOLDER IN Interface\AddOns\ ***

The addon is the other download (1-ADDON-LevelPace.zip). It goes into
Interface\AddOns and works entirely on its own.

This folder is the COMPANION: a small program that runs outside the game and
carries your stats to the leaderboard, because a WoW addon cannot use the
internet. Put this folder anywhere. Your desktop is fine.


WHAT TO DO
----------

  1. Double-click   LevelPace Companion.bat
     An icon appears in the notification area (bottom-right, maybe under
     the little ^ arrow). Nothing installs. It uses the PowerShell that is
     already in Windows.

  2. In game, turn sharing on, once:
       click the LevelPace minimap button  ->  Share to leaderboard
       (or type:  /lp share on)

  3. Log out, or type /reload.
     WoW writes its saved data only then -- never while you play -- and the
     companion uploads within seconds of it doing so.

That is it. Leave the icon running while you play.


IF SOMETHING IS OFF
-------------------

  "Could not find your World of Warcraft folder"
      Right-click the icon -> Set WoW folder... -> pick the folder that
      contains Wow.exe.

  Anything else
      Right-click the icon -> Copy log (for Discord) -> paste it to whoever
      gave you this. Or run "LevelPace Companion (SHOW ERRORS).bat", which
      keeps its window open and prints the problem.
